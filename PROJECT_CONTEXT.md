## 2. Core company idea

The simplest explanation of HAX Systems is:

> "Every business needs a system. We build it."

This is the primary company philosophy.

HAX Systems does NOT start by trying to sell a particular technology. We start by understanding a business.

We examine:

- how customers discover the business
- how enquiries arrive
- how enquiries are answered
- how bookings happen
- how leads are recorded
- how customers are followed up
- how employees perform repetitive tasks
- how data is stored
- how communication happens
- how customers return
- where opportunities are being lost
- where unnecessary manual work exists

Then we determine: **WHAT DIGITAL SYSTEM WOULD MAKE THIS BUSINESS WORK BETTER?**

Only after understanding the problem do we recommend or build technology. The technology is the solution. The business problem comes first.

## 6. Business problems we solve

HAX Systems should talk about customer problems before talking about technology.

Typical problems include:

- **Missed enquiries** — Customers call or message but nobody responds quickly.
- **Missed calls** — Employees are working and cannot answer every call.
- **Weak website** — A website exists but produces few enquiries or bookings.
- **No website** — A business does not have a credible digital presence.
- **Outdated website** — The site is slow, confusing, dated or difficult to use.
- **Poor customer journey** — Visitors do not know what to do next.
- **Manual booking** — Appointments are handled through calls, spreadsheets or messages.
- **Slow follow-up** — Potential customers are contacted too late.
- **No follow-up** — Customers disappear after using the service.
- **Repetitive administration** — Employees spend unnecessary hours on routine tasks.
- **Inconsistent social media** — The business wants to stay visible but does not post consistently.
- **Disconnected tools** — The business uses several systems that do not work together.
- **No central customer system** — Customer information is scattered across phones, spreadsheets, inboxes and messaging platforms.
- **Weak reporting** — The owner cannot easily understand leads, bookings, revenue or activity.

HAX Systems identifies these kinds of problems and recommends the appropriate system.

## 8. Systems consulting / System Review

The company should have a central entry point called: **SYSTEM REVIEW**

This is important because many customers do not know what technology they need. And they should not be expected to know.

The customer's job is to understand their business. Our job is to understand systems.

The System Review should answer questions such as:

- How does the business currently get customers?

- How are calls handled?
- How does booking work?
- Where is staff time being wasted?
- What process is repetitive?
- What frequently gets forgotten?

## 9. HAX methodology

Use a five-step methodology.

- **01 — Review:** Understand how the business currently works.
- **02 — Identify:** Find where customers, opportunities, money or staff time are being lost.
- **03 — Design:** Determine what system should solve the problem.
- **04 — Build:** Design, develop, integrate and implement the system.
- **05 — Improve:** Refine and improve it as the business grows.

This methodology should appear visually on the website.

Important principle:

> "We don't start with technology. We start with your business."
